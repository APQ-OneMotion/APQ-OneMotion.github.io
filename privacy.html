Privacy Policy — QB-Agent

Effective date: September 3, 2026

QB-Agent is an internal software tool operated by Actionpaq Corporation, 2120 Town W Dr., Rogers, AR, USA. It connects to Actionpaq Corporation's own QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's API to support internal accounting and reporting.

What data the tool accesses

QB-Agent reads accounting records from Actionpaq Corporation's QuickBooks Online company: customers, vendors, invoices, bills, payments, and financial reports. All records belong to Actionpaq Corporation. The tool does not access data belonging to any other company or individual outside Actionpaq Corporation's business records.

How data is used

Data is used for Actionpaq Corporation's internal accounting, reporting, and business operations. Access is limited to authorized Actionpaq Corporation employees.

What is not done with data

  • Data is not sold.
  • Data is not shared with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • Data is not used for marketing.

Storage and security

Credentials and tokens are stored on Actionpaq Corporation-controlled systems with access limited to authorized personnel. Connections to Intuit's API use TLS encryption.

Data retention and removal

Actionpaq Corporation retains its own business records under its normal record-keeping practices. Disconnecting the app from QuickBooks Online revokes its access; revocation is available at any time through Intuit's app management settings.
